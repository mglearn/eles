What does good learning look like today?

72 ready-to-run activities built on the TCEA Essential Learning Expectations: 37 for professional learning and 35 for K–16 classrooms. Each one weaves in Gen AI literacy, digital citizenship, or media literacy. Every activity comes with print-ready handouts, and 33 need no devices at all.

Technology use by itself is not evidence of learning. Each activity names the evidence to look for, and says plainly whether a screen earns its place.
