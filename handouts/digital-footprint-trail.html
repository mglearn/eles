Digital Footprint Trail

Teams follow a trail of paper footprints left online by a fictional fourth grader, piece together what a stranger could learn about him, and plan the footprints they want to leave.

Every post, comment, photo, quiz answer, and chatbot message leaves a digital footprint, and footprints add up. In this walk-around mystery, teams follow twelve paper footprints left by Kai Morales, a fictional fourth grader. No single post seems like a big deal. Put together, they reveal Kai's school, street, house number, the hint to his password, and when he's home alone. The trail also includes footprints Kai can be proud of. Teams then give Kai a footprint makeover and plan their own trail, including what they will and won't type into AI tools.

In this packet

  • Handout A: Kai's Footprint Cards (cut-apart cards)
  • Handout B: Trail Log (organizer)
  • Handout C: My Footprint Plan (worksheet)
  • Facilitator key (last page)

TCEA ELE indicators

  • S2.3 I am aware of the potential risks of sharing content online.
  • S2.1 I know how to protect my personal information online.
  • S4.3 I am aware of my learning strengths and areas for improvement.
  • AI2.1 I know how to apply ethical principles when using or developing AI for education.

Facilitator only for Digital Footprint Trail

Answer key and notes

Handout A: Kai's Footprint Cards

Footprint 1 · Video
Reveals: his school and grade. Who might see: anyone the video is shared with. Risky. Better: film somewhere without the sign.
Footprint 2 · Comment
Reveals: pet's name AND a password hint. Risky. Passwords shouldn't be based on anything people can find out about you.
Footprint 3 · Photo
Reveals: his house number. Combined with the street from Footprint 9, someone could find his home. Risky. Better: crop it or take the photo in a park.
Footprint 4 · Post
Reveals: when he's alone. Risky. Schedules and "home alone" posts stay private.
Footprint 5 · Comment
Hurtful. Everyone who sees the drawing sees the comment, and it tells people how Kai treats others.
Footprint 6 · Photo
Hurtful and risky. It's Tomas's footprint too, and he didn't agree. Ask first.
Footprint 7 · Review
Helpful. It shares an opinion and helps other readers, with no private information.
Footprint 8 · Video
Helpful. It shows his creativity and teaches others, without private details.
Footprint 9 · Quiz
Risky. Quizzes can collect information. Now his street is out there. A fun quiz doesn't need your real details.
Footprint 10 · Chatbot
Risky. What you type into apps and chatbots may be saved. He could get the same help by leaving out his name and street.
Footprint 11 · Deleted
Deleting doesn't always erase a footprint. Screenshots and shares can keep it around. Pause before you post.
Footprint 12 · Post
Helpful. It shows kindness and community, with no private information.
