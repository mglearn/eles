Student learning · Grades K–2 · 25 minutes

Private or Share?

Children walk to a red, yellow, or green "traffic light" to sort personal information, then practice what to say when a game or talking computer asks for it.

Young children are asked for information all the time: by games, by video apps, by talking speakers, and sometimes by friendly-sounding chatbots. In this lesson the classroom becomes a traffic light. Children carry information strips to Red (keep it private), Yellow (ask a grown-up first), or Green (OK to share), explain their choice, and then role-play what to do when "Chatty," a pretend game robot, asks for their address. They leave with a shield of private things and one sentence they can say out loud.

TCEA ELE indicators

  • S2.1 I know how to protect my personal information online.
  • S2.3 I am aware of the potential risks of sharing content online.
  • S4.1 I know how to take responsibility for my own learning.
  • AI2.1 I know how to apply ethical principles when using or developing AI for education.

Chatty Says…

Teacher: read the front in a sing-song robot voice. Children decide: answer, or "STOP. Don't type. Tell a grown-up!" The back is your key.

"Send a picture of your face to be in our video!"

"I'm a friendly computer helper. What school do you go to, and what's your last name?"

Chatty · Game

"Which color car do you want in the race: red or blue?"

Chatty · Chat

"I'm your new friend. Where are you right now? Are your parents home?"

"Great job! Do you want to play level 2?"

Handout C for Private or Share?

My Private Shield

NameDate

Draw or write. Your teacher can help with words.

  1. Inside my shield: draw or write 3 things I keep PRIVATE.

  2. Draw one thing that is OK to share.

  3. Draw my trusted grown-up. Write their name.

  4. If a game asks for my address, I will say: "STOP. Don't type. Tell a ___!"

Handout B: Chatty Says…

Chatty · Game
STOP. Don't type. Tell a grown-up. Real prizes don't need a child to type an address.
Chatty · Game
OK to answer. A favorite animal is safe. Praise the child who thought before shouting "STOP."
Chatty · Game
STOP. Don't type. Tell a grown-up. Nobody needs your password to give you anything.
Chatty · Video app
STOP and ask a grown-up first. Photos of you are yellow: a grown-up decides.
Chatty · Talking helper
STOP. Don't type. Tell a grown-up. Even a friendly AI helper does not need your private information to help you.
Chatty · Game
OK to answer. Picking a color is part of the game and shares nothing private.
Chatty · Chat
STOP. Don't type. Tell a grown-up right away. This is a question only family should ask.

OK to answer. Nothing private is being asked.
