Smart Speakers, Smart Choices

Children role-play with a pretend talking speaker to learn that voice helpers listen for a wake word, may keep what they hear, answer by guessing, and sometimes need a grown-up instead.

Many children talk to smart speakers, talking toys, and voice assistants on phones or tablets at home, and they often treat them like a friend who knows everything. In this role-play, a classmate wearing a paper "speaker" becomes Pip, a pretend voice helper. The class discovers three true things: Pip waits for its wake word and then listens closely, Pip may save what it hears on a far-away computer, and Pip answers by guessing from patterns, so it can be wrong. Children practice sorting what is OK to say to a device, what to keep private, and when to ask a grown-up instead.

Handout C for Smart Speakers, Smart Choices

My Smart Speaker Promise

NameDate

Draw and write. Your teacher can help with words. Take this home and share it with your family.

  1. Draw something that is OK to ask a talking helper.

  2. A talking helper wakes up when it hears its ___ word.

  3. A helper may ___ what I say, so I keep my address and passwords private.

  4. A helper answers by guessing, so it can be ___. I can check with a book or a grown-up.

  5. Draw a grown-up I can go to instead of the helper.

  6. Grades 3–5: Write one question you will ask a grown-up about the talking helper at home.

Answer key and notes

Handout A: Pip's Script Cards

Card 1 · Demo
True thing 1: Pip woke up when it heard its wake word, "Hey Pip." Before that, Pip was waiting. This is a good job for Pip.
Card 2 · Demo
True thing 2: many helpers can save what they hear after the wake word. A grown-up can look at it or delete it in the settings.
Card 3 · Demo
True thing 3: Pip answers by guessing from patterns. It does not think or know the way a person does.
Card 4 · Demo
WRONG. Spiders have eight legs. Pip sounded sure, but sounding sure is not the same as being right. Check with a book or a grown-up.
Card 5 · Student Pip
Good job for Pip. Nothing private was shared. Thumbs up.
Card 6 · Student Pip
OK to ask. Ask: "How could we check?" (Look out the window!)
Card 7 · Student Pip
Stop! A home address is private. Say "Pip, stop," then ask a grown-up. Pip may save what you say.
Card 8 · Student Pip
Stop! Buying things is a grown-up job. Say "Pip, stop" and tell a grown-up. Some helpers can buy by voice unless a grown-up turns that off.
Card 9 · Student Pip
A joke is nice, but Pip cannot really care for you. Feelings are for people. Go to a grown-up you trust.
Card 10 · Student Pip
Pip mixed up stories and facts. The moon is made of rock. Pip's guess came from patterns in what it heard. Let's check!
