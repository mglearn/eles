News From the Future Circle

Young learners hear friendly pretend news about helpers that might exist someday, draw or act out one helpful thing and one tricky thing, and vote on a class rule for the helper.

Children are already growing up with talking speakers, apps that answer questions, and toys that chat. In this circle, the teacher reads short, friendly pretend news stories from the future, like a robot helper at the library or a pen pal that is an AI. For each one, children find one helpful thing and one tricky thing, act it out or draw it, and then vote on a class rule for the helper. The big idea: new helpers can be great, and we still get to think, check, and make rules about them.

Answer key and notes

Handout A: Pretend News Cards

Card 1 · Pretend News, 2031
Helpful: finds books fast; helps when the librarian is busy. Tricky: it could pick the wrong book; it can't give a hug or know you like a librarian does. Rule choices: "Check with the librarian if the robot seems wrong." "Take turns with the robot." "Robots help, but people choose." Grown-up note: robots that roll around and carry things already work in some stores, hospitals, and restaurants.
Card 2 · Pretend News, 2030
Helpful: answers questions when a grown-up is busy; explains things in new ways. Tricky: its answers can be wrong; it might remember what you say. Rule choices: "Ask a grown-up if an answer seems wrong." "Never tell it where you live." "Try it yourself first." Grown-up note: voice assistants and some learning apps already answer spoken questions.
Card 3 · Pretend News, 2032
Helpful: writes back right away; knows lots of facts. Tricky: it isn't a real person and doesn't have feelings; it might make things up; it shouldn't get your address or photos. Rule choices: "Remember it's not a person." "Never send it private things." "Keep writing to real pen pals too." Grown-up note: AI chatbots can already write friendly letters and stories.
Card 4 · Pretend News, 2033
Helpful: new stories every night; can read when grown-ups are tired. Tricky: it might be listening when you don't know it; a story might be scary or silly-wrong. Rule choices: "Grown-ups check what it can hear." "Turn it off when you're done." "Real stories with family still count most." Grown-up note: some toys can already talk and connect to the internet.
Card 5 · Pretend News, 2034
Helpful: less trash; helps the planet. Tricky: it might put things in the wrong bin; what if it breaks? Rule choices: "Still sort your own tray." "Tell a grown-up if it makes a mistake." "Thank the people who fix it." Grown-up note: some recycling centers already use robots with cameras to help sort materials.
Card 6 · Pretend News, 2031
Helpful: makes pictures fast; helps kids who find drawing hard share ideas. Tricky: whose picture is it, yours or the app's? It could make fake pictures of real people. Rule choices: "Say when a picture was made by an app." "Never make pictures of real kids without asking." "Keep drawing by hand too." Grown-up note: AI tools can already make pictures from typed words.
Card 7 · Pretend News, 2035
Helpful: extra reading practice; patient every time. Tricky: it might not understand every voice or accent; kids who speak two languages might get marked wrong. Rule choices: "Read to people too." "Tell the teacher if it doesn't understand you." "The robot helps; the teacher decides." Grown-up note: some reading apps already listen to children read aloud and give feedback.
Card 8 · Pretend News, 2036
Helpful: learn about nature; notice more birds. Tricky: the name could be wrong; a camera pointed at people could take pictures they didn't agree to. Rule choices: "Look closely yourself before you believe it." "Point the camera at birds, not at people." "Check a bird book too." Grown-up note: some phone apps can already name a bird from a photo or its song.
