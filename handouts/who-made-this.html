Student learning · Grades K–2 · 30 minutes

Who Made This?

Children discover that every picture, video, song, and game was made by someone for a reason, and practice asking "Who made this, and why?"

Young children often experience media as something that just appears on a screen or a shelf. In this lesson they meet the makers behind it. You describe familiar kinds of media (a picture book, a cereal box, a cartoon, a game, a school poster, a picture made with an AI tool), and children answer two detective questions: Who made this? (a person, a company, or a person using AI) and Why did they make it? (to teach, to sell, to have fun, or to tell news). Children walk to "why" corners, then become makers themselves. The big idea they carry home: every piece of media was made by someone, on purpose, and good makers are honest about how they made it.

TCEA ELE indicators

  • S2.2 I can identify trustworthy sources of information on the internet.
  • S1.3 I am aware of how AI can assist me in my studies.
  • AI2.3 I am aware of the importance of transparency and accountability in AI systems.
  • S3.2 I can communicate my ideas clearly to others.

Handout B for Who Made This?

I Am a Maker

NameDate

Draw and write. Your teacher can help you with words.

  1. Draw something YOU would make: a poster, a comic, a card, or a game.

  2. Who is it for?

  3. Why did I make it? Circle one: TEACH · SELL · FUN · NEWS

  4. What did I use to make it? (crayons, a camera, a computer, my voice…)

  5. Draw a maker you know: a person who makes books, songs, pictures, or games.

Facilitator only for Who Made This?

Answer key and notes

Handout A: Maker Cards

Card 1 · Book
WHO: an author who wrote the words and an illustrator who drew the pictures. WHY: TEACH (and fun). Use this one to model.
Card 2 · Box
WHO: a cereal company. WHY: SELL. The cartoon is there so kids notice the box and ask for it.
Card 3 · Cartoon
WHO: a team of writers, artists, and voice actors. WHY: FUN.
Card 4 · Poster
WHO: the school nurse. WHY: TEACH. It helps keep everyone healthy.
Card 5 · Video
WHO: a toy company. WHY: SELL. It's fun to watch, but the biggest reason is to get you to want the toy.
Card 6 · Card
WHO: one person, the big sister. WHY: FUN (and to say "I love you"). Not every maker is a company.
Card 7 · News
WHO: a news team. WHY: NEWS. It tells people what's happening so they can bring an umbrella.
Card 8 · Game
WHO: a game company with designers and artists. WHY: TEACH and FUN. Ask: "Does it ask you to buy anything? Then it might be SELL too."
Card 9 · Song
WHO: a songwriter and singer. WHY: TEACH (and fun).
Card 10 · News
WHO: teachers and students at the school. WHY: NEWS. It tells families what happened.
Card 11 · Made with AI
WHO: Mr. Okafor, using an AI tool. The tool made the picture from patterns it learned; Mr. Okafor decided to make it. WHY: FUN (to make reading cozy).
Card 12 · Made with AI
Talk about the label. Good makers tell the truth about how they made something, just like writing your name on your drawing.
