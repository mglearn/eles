What Your Map App Knows

Students plot a fictional teen's Saturday from location clues on a town map, discover how much a trail of pings reveals, weigh safety against privacy, and write a location settings checkup plan.

Phones, games, cameras, and friend-finder apps can record where we are, often without us thinking about it. In this unplugged simulation, teams receive twelve location clues from one Saturday in the life of Dani, a fictional seventh grader in the fictional town of Juniper Falls: map app pings, a geotagged photo, a check-in, a live location share. Teams plot the trail on a printed town grid and discover that a handful of dots reveals where Dani lives, who Dani hangs out with, and where Dani will probably be next Saturday. Then students weigh when sharing location keeps people safe and when it puts them at risk, and write a step-by-step checkup plan for their own device settings.

TCEA ELE indicators

  • S2.1 I know how to protect my personal information online.
  • S2.3 I am aware of the potential risks of sharing content online.
  • S5.1 I know how to approach complex problems systematically.

Answer key and notes

Handout B: Dani's Saturday Location Clues

Location history · 12:10 AM
Where a phone sits overnight is almost always home. This is the clue Dani never saw being created.
Map app · 9:05 AM
Someone drove Dani to soccer. Directions requests reveal both the start point and the destination.
Check-in · 9:20 AM
Dani chose this one on purpose. It shows the team name, too, which tells people where Dani will be every Saturday morning.
Map app suggestion · 8:40 AM
The phone predicted the trip from Dani's past Saturdays. The pattern of past locations lets software guess future ones.
Live location share · 11:30 AM
Dani and Rae share live location. That's a choice between friends, but anyone on Dani's list can see where Dani is right now.
Geotagged photo · 11:45 AM
Many cameras can save location inside a photo file. Some apps remove it when you post; others keep it. Worth checking your camera setting.
Game · 1:15 PM
The game had location set to "always." Dani wasn't even playing. Apps with "always" permission can collect location all day.
Photo post · 1:40 PM
Someone else shared Dani's location. You can't control every post, but you can talk with friends about tagging.
Map app · 3:10 PM
Search history and walking directions add another dot. Together with the others, a full day is visible.
Location history · 4:30 PM
A long stop at a house in the afternoon suggests a friend's or relative's home. Location history saves places you stay a while.
Live location share · 7:05 PM
Family sharing can be a real safety tool. It also confirms the home location from Card 1.
Location history · 11:00 PM
Same place, same time, week after week. Now anyone who saw this trail could guess where Dani lives and where Dani will be next Saturday at 9:20 AM.
