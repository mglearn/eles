# TCEA Essential Learning Expectations (ELEs)

A common language for better learning across five roles — **Teachers & Teacher Librarians, Students, School Leaders & Administrators, Instructional Coaches,** and **AI in Education**. Each role has five key areas with three indicators each, written as "I know," "I can," and "I am aware." Every indicator includes an example activity and its standards correlation.

# Teachers & Teacher Librarians

**Guiding Questions**
- What strategies am I using to build positive relationships with my students?
- How am I fostering critical thinking skills in my lessons?
- How effectively am I integrating technology into my teaching practices?
- In what ways am I promoting digital literacy among my students?
- How am I creating an inclusive learning environment that respects diversity?
