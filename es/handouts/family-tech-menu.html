The Family Tech Menu

A child and their family sort a week of real screen moments by what the screen was for, then build a balanced "tech menu" together, learning that purpose and mix matter more than minutes.

A lot of screen time happens on autopilot: the show that was already on, the game you open without thinking. This lesson gives families a calmer way to talk about screen use than counting minutes. Children sort everyday screen moments into four purposes, Create, Connect, Learn, and Unwind, and notice that a screen can earn its place or just fill time. They discover that a device used to make a video for Grandma is not the same as one used because there was nothing else to do, even if both take the same number of minutes. Then, at home, the child leads the family in building a "tech menu": a short, balanced plan of screen and no-screen choices for the week. The message is honest and nuanced: screens are not the enemy, and more is not automatically bad; what the screen is for, and what else the week holds, is what counts.

Hoja B de The Family Tech Menu

Our Family Tech Menu

NombreFecha

Start this at school with one idea in each purpose. Finish it at home: decide the menu together with your family, who can add, change, or trade ideas. Family note: this is not a list of bans. We are practicing choosing screen use on purpose and balancing it with no-screen time. Please build this with your child.

  1. CREATE: one thing we could make with a screen this week (a video, a slideshow, a playlist, a drawing).

  2. CONNECT: one way we could use a screen to be with people we love this week.

  3. LEARN: one thing we could find out or practice with a screen this week.

  4. UNWIND: one show, game, or video we will pick on purpose, and when we will stop.

  5. NO-SCREEN: two things our family will do this week with no screens at all.

  6. One screen-free time or place we choose together (like dinner or the hour before bed):
