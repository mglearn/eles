Daily Opener a Day

A five-minute opener for every school day, one Student ELE each week. Flip to today, choose K–5 or 6–12, and project it.

The calendar follows a typical Texas 2026–27 school year: weekends, Labor Day, Thanksgiving week, winter break, MLK Day, Presidents Day, spring break, and Good Friday are skipped. Set your own first day, or turn holidays off and use the arrows, to match your district.
